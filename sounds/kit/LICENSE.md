# Drum samples: CC BY-SA 3.0

The drum sounds in this folder (`kit.webm`, `kit.m4a`, and the take list `kit.json`) are adapted from the
**Salamander Drumkit by Alexander Holm**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

- Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderDrumkit (`salamanderDrumkit.tar.bz2`)
- Changes: a selection of takes, mixed to mono at 48 kHz, the silence before each hit removed, each ring-out
  faded at its end, levels matched across the kit, compressed to Opus and AAC, and joined into one file.
  Built with `tools/make-kit.js`.

These adapted samples stay under **CC BY-SA 3.0**: you may share and change them, as long as you credit
"Salamander Drumkit by Alexander Holm" and share your changed samples under the same licence.

and other files) is not covered by it.
